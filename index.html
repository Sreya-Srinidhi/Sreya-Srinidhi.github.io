Biography

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at Case Western Reserve University (GPA 4.0), pursuing an AI focus with an early-stage thesis in multimodal machine learning at the VULab under Dr. Yu Yin. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision and representation learning — currently exploring Gaussian splatting–based representations for scene segmentation.


Alongside my thesis I evaluate out-of-distribution detection models on financial time-series data with Dr. Vipin Chaudhary's group. Previously, I interned at Publicis Sapient building an AI-assisted automotive chatbot with LangChain/LangGraph, and at Ubihere researching multi-scene person re-identification.


Outside research I lead the CWRU competitive badminton team (advancing it from Division 3 to Division 1 in two years) and serve as Vice President of the Tau Beta Pi Ohio Alpha Chapter. I hold an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot License and a Veale Snyder Entrepreneurship Fellowship.

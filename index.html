Research interests

About

I’m completing my master’s thesis in VULab at CWRU, advised by Yu Yin, and working with Matthew O’Toole in the Computational Imaging Group at Carnegie Mellon University. My thesis connects object-aware 3D reconstruction with an original audio-visual extension that I am implementing for acoustic synthesis.

I’m interested in multimodal learning broadly. Audio is the modality I’m currently investigating alongside vision, within a wider interest in representations of the physical world.

Research projects

Sep 2025–May 2026

Reintroducing Nuisance Variables for Image Reconstruction

Developed a Dual-SAL framework to study whether nuisance variables removed from minimally sufficient visual representations can be deliberately reintroduced for controlled image reconstruction. Built a SAL-based encoder, nuisance estimator, and generative decoder, investigating viewpoint and lighting. Explored homomorphic illumination–reflectance separation, geometry-inspired filtering, and denoising-style generative modeling. Identified limitations in treating lighting as frequency-separable illumination or additive corruption, motivating physically grounded representation learning.

Exploratory research · Representation learning · Image reconstruction

Oct–Dec 2025

SelectiveSR: Super Resolution Using 2D Gaussian Splatting

Developed a hybrid arbitrary-scale super-resolution framework combining local specialization from Gaussian Splatting with global generalization via ContinuousSR’s Deep Gaussian Prior, achieving higher PSNR than bicubic interpolation and recent Gaussian SR baselines.

Group project with Gautam Kakadiya and Alan Luo

Feb–May 2025

Lung Cancer Prediction from Gene Expression

Conducted exploratory modeling on TCGA-LUAD gene expression data using ICA, manifold learning, and clustering to analyze latent structure, achieving 88% classification accuracy.

Exploratory modeling · Gene expression
